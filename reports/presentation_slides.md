### SLIDE 2: The Problem: Why Old Anti-Spoofing Systems Fail
- **The Generative Shift:** Speech synthesis has evolved from older vocoders (Tacotron, HiFi-GAN) to **Diffusion Models** (Grad-TTS) and **Flow-Matching ODEs** (Matcha-TTS, Voicebox, Seed-TTS).
- **The Shocking Reality:** Traditional models trained on ASVspoof 2019 experience an Equal Error Rate of **over 40%** against modern diffusion speech!
- **Why?** Older systems look for metallic vocoder buzz and phase distortion—artifacts that continuous-time diffusion models do not produce.
> **Speaker Notes:**  
> "The audio deepfake landscape has fundamentally changed. If you test an older detector trained on ASVspoof against modern AI voices generated by ElevenLabs, Matcha-TTS, or Voicebox, it fails over 40% of the time. Our project solves this critical security gap."

### SLIDE 4: Acoustic Feature Extraction: Log-Mel Spectrograms
- **Feature Choice:** 128-band Log-Mel Spectrograms normalized to $[0, 1]$.
- **Acoustic Signatures of Diffusion Models:**
  - **Score-based Denoising Noise:** Diffusion models leave microscopic variance in upper frequency bands due to finite reverse steps.
  - **Flow-Matching Discretization:** ODE solver step trajectories create subtle transition artifacts at phoneme boundaries.
- **Input Tensor:** Standardized to $(128 \times 94 \times 1)$ covering 3.0 seconds at 16 kHz.
> **Speaker Notes:**  
> "By transforming audio into a 128-band Log-Mel Spectrogram, we enable our deep learning model to inspect the high-frequency score residuals and flow-matching trajectories that distinguish AI speech from natural human voice."

---

### SLIDE 5: The Hybrid CNN-LSTM Neural Network Architecture
- **Why CNN + LSTM?**
  - **2D CNN Front-End (3 Conv2D Blocks):** Extracts spatial-spectral patterns and diffusion noise floors across frequency bands.
  - **Reshape & Permute:** Preserves 47 sequential time frames while expanding features to 2048 dimensions per step.
  - **Stacked Bidirectional LSTM (64 + 32 Units):** Models forward and backward temporal flow dynamics and phoneme transition coherence.
  - **Classification Head:** Dense(64) $\to$ Dropout(0.4) $\to$ Dense(1, Sigmoid) outputting $P(\text{Spoof})$.
> **Speaker Notes:**  
> "Our architecture is uniquely suited for continuous-time generative models: the CNN detects spatial diffusion noise across frequency bins, while the Bidirectional LSTM detects temporal boundary inconsistencies across speech frames."

---

### SLIDE 6: Training & Regularization Strategy
- **Optimization:** Adam ($\alpha = 0.001$) with Binary Cross-Entropy Loss.
- **Target Tensor Alignment:** Strict $(N, 1)$ target shaping for numerical stability.
- **Callbacks & Overfitting Prevention:**
  - Batch Normalization after every convolution.
  - Spatial & recurrent Dropout ($0.2$ to $0.4$).
  - `ModelCheckpoint` saving optimal weights on validation loss.
  - `EarlyStopping` (patience = 5) and `ReduceLROnPlateau`.
> **Speaker Notes:**  
> "We implemented rigorous regularizations to ensure the model learns generalized diffusion patterns rather than memorizing individual speaker identities."

### SLIDE 8: Forensic Diagnostics & Multi-Modal Scoring
- In addition to the deep neural network, our system computes:
  - **Spectral Centroid Drift:** Gauges natural human prosodic pitch variation vs. synthetic rigidity.
  - **High-Frequency Diffusion Energy Ratio:** Detects vocoder/diffusion denoising noise floor.
  - **Spectral Flatness:** Identifies residual Gaussian noise in unvoiced speech frames.
> **Speaker Notes:**  
> "We also incorporate physical acoustic metrics into an ensemble score, ensuring the system remains transparent and explainable to security operators."
